Vela — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Vela is an accessibility app that shows large, simple home-screen widgets: a clock, a one-tap call to a trusted contact, a medication reminder, and a daily check-in that can let a family member know the person is well. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and what it never does.

What stays only on the device

Most of what you enter never leaves your phone. It is kept in the app's private storage and is not uploaded to us or to anyone else:

DataWhy
Trusted contact's name and phone numberSo the Call widget can dial them.
Medication name and timeTo show the reminder widget.
Whether today's medication was marked as takenTo show the confirmation on the widget.
Chosen accent colourAppearance only.

What is shared, and only when you pair with a family member

If — and only if — you create a pairing code to connect with someone who looks after you, a single record is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, hosted in the European Union:

DataWhy
The pairing codeIdentifies the connection between the two phones.
The name you typed for yourselfSo your family member sees who the status belongs to.
Date and time of each check-inSo your family member can see you are well.
The hour by which a check-in is expectedTo know when an alert is due.
Two anonymous identifiersRandomly generated by Google Firebase, one per phone, to tell the two devices apart. They are not linked to your name, email, or Google account.

Your phone number, your contact's phone number, and your medication details are never uploaded, not even when paired.

Notifications

When a check-in has not happened by the expected hour, a scheduled job running in the European Union sends a push notification to the paired family member's phone. This uses OneSignal, which receives only the anonymous identifier of the device to notify and the name shown in the message.

Purchases

Optional paid features are handled by RevenueCat together with the app store. We never see or store your payment details.

Who else processes this data

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication) — stores the pairing record and issues the anonymous identifiers.
  • OneSignal — delivers the push notification to the family member.
  • RevenueCat — manages purchases and entitlements.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

How long it is kept

Device-only data lasts until you delete it or uninstall the app. The pairing record is kept while the connection exists. To have a pairing record deleted, contact us with your pairing code and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data, or to stop processing it. Because the pairing record holds no name, email, or phone number beyond the display name you chose, the pairing code is what lets us find it. Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Vela is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or share, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's store listing.
