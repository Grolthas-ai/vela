Vela — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Vela is an accessibility app that shows large, simple home-screen widgets: a clock, a one-tap call to a trusted contact, medication, a daily check-in that lets a family member know the person is well, and a help button that tells the family when help is needed. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and what it never does.

The app has two kinds of user: the person who is looked after, and one or more family members who look after them. Where it matters, this policy says which of the two it is talking about.

What stays only on the device

This is kept in the app's private storage and is not uploaded to us or to anyone else:

DataWhy
Trusted contacts' names and phone numbersSo the Call widget can dial them.
Your medicines: name, amount, time, how often, and whether each was taken todayTo show the medication widget. They stay on the device unless you choose to show them to your family — see below.
Chosen colours and clock styleAppearance only.

Vela does not include this data in Android's cloud backup or in the copy made when you move to a new phone. A new phone starts empty and is connected again with a new code.

What is shared when you connect with a family member

If — and only if — you create a code to connect with someone who looks after you, a record is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, hosted in the European Union. You and the family members connected to you can read it. So can anyone who has the code, until a family member has connected — and for as long as a subscription lets more family members join — which is why the code should only be given to people you trust. It holds:

DataWhy
The codeIdentifies the connection between the phones.
The name you typed for yourselfSo your family sees who the status belongs to. It also appears in the notifications they receive.
The date and time of your latest check-inSo your family can see you are well.
The days on which you checked in during the last two weeksSo your family can see how the last days have gone, not only today.
When your phone last connected, and its battery level at that momentSo your family can tell a phone that is switched off or flat from a day you have simply not checked in yet.
The hour by which a check-in is expected, and your phone's time zoneTo know when an alert is due, at the right hour where you are.
Requests for help: when you pressed the help button, whether the alert reached your family, and who answered and whenTo alert your family, and to show you honestly whether they have been told.
The names your family members typed for themselvesSo you can see who will be told, and who is coming.
One anonymous identifier per phoneRandomly generated by Google Firebase to tell the devices apart. They are not linked to your name, email, or Google account.
Whether a subscription covers the connection, and the anonymous identifier of whoever pays for itTo switch the paid features on for the family.

Your phone number and your contacts' phone numbers are never uploaded, not even when connected.

Your medicines: shared only if you say yes

What somebody takes is health information, so Vela asks before sending it anywhere. On the Medications screen the person who is looked after is asked whether to show their medicines to their family. Until they say yes, nothing about medication leaves the phone.

If they say yes, the following is stored alongside the connection, readable only by the family members connected to them:

DataWhy
Each medicine's name, amount, time and how often it is takenSo the family can see what is due.
For each day, which medicines were due and when each was marked as takenSo the family can see whether a dose was missed, and for the weekly summary.

This can be switched off at any time on the same screen. Switching it off deletes the medicines and the day-by-day record from our servers, not only stops new ones being sent.

Notifications

When a check-in has not happened by the expected hour, or when the help button is pressed, a function running in the European Union sends a push notification to the connected family members. This uses OneSignal, which receives the anonymous identifier of each phone to notify, the text of the message (which includes the name you typed for yourself), and the connection's code so the app can open on the right person.

Purchases

Optional paid features are handled by Google Play together with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives the anonymous identifier of the phone that subscribed and the details of the subscription, and tells us whether it is active. We never see or store your payment details.

Who else processes this data

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Functions) — stores the connection record in the European Union and issues the anonymous identifiers.
  • OneSignal — delivers the push notifications. OneSignal is based in the United States and may process this data there.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions. RevenueCat is based in the United States and may process this data there.

These companies act on our instructions as service providers. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising or profiling, and the app contains no advertising.

How long it is kept

  • Data that stays on the device lasts until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • The connection record is kept until you ask us to delete it. Uninstalling the app does not delete it.
  • A family member who chooses "Disconnect" is removed from the record straight away and stops receiving notifications.
  • Shared medicines and their day-by-day record are kept while sharing is on, and deleted when it is switched off.

To have a connection record deleted, write to the address below with the code and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data, or to stop processing it, and you can withdraw your agreement to share your medicines at any time in the app. Because the record holds no email or phone number, the code is what lets us find it. Write to the address below and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Vela is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or share, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's store listing.
