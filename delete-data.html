Vela — Delete your data

This page explains how to have the data held by Vela — Check in on family (developer: Grolthas) deleted. Vela has no accounts, so there is nothing to close: what exists is the record that connects a person with the family members who look after them.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Open Vela on the phone of the person who is looked after and note the six-character code shown under "Your code". On a family member's phone, the same code is shown at the bottom of that person's screen.
  2. Send an email to gasperiniricardo@gmail.com with the subject "Vela – delete my data" and that code.
  3. We delete the record and confirm by email, within 30 days.

The code is what lets us find the record, because it holds no email address or phone number.

What is deleted

  • The connection record: the code, the name typed in the app, check-in times, the expected check-in hour and time zone, requests for help and who answered them, the names the family members typed, and the anonymous identifiers of the phones.
  • The medicines and the day-by-day record of what was taken, if they were shared.

What you can delete yourself, in the app

  • Medicines shared with your family: Medications → "Stop showing them". This deletes them from our servers straight away.
  • A family member's connection: on their phone, "Disconnect". They are removed from the record straight away.
  • Everything kept only on the phone (contacts, medicines, settings): uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android's settings.

What is kept

  • Nothing from the connection record is kept after deletion.
  • Subscription records are held by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider, against an anonymous identifier. Ask in the same email and we will have RevenueCat's record deleted too. Google Play keeps its own purchase history under your Google account.
